# UX Notes

## Core Philosophy

Ingredo is not a task manager.

Shopping items are not tasks.

Avoid checkboxes whenever possible.

## Shopping Interaction

Active Shopping List:

- Milk
- Cheese
- Bread

User taps Milk.

Result:

Recently Purchased:

- Milk

Users can tap Milk again to restore it.

## Recently Purchased Purpose

The Recently Purchased section serves as:

1. Purchase history
2. Undo mechanism
3. Quick-add library

## Navigation

Recommended tabs:

- Recipes
- Week
- Shop
- Settings

## Home Screen Exploration

Explore three concepts:

### Concept A

Shopping-first

### Concept B

Meal-plan-first

### Concept C

Recipe-first

Current hypothesis:

Meal-plan-first will best differentiate Ingredo from competing apps.

## Design Direction

- Warm
- Calm
- Kitchen-inspired
- One-handed usage
- Large touch targets
- Grocery-store friendly
- Minimal clutter
