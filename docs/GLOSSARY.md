# Glossary

## Recipe

A saved cooking instruction with ingredients and preparation steps.

## Ingredient

A single food item used in a recipe.

## Meal Plan

A schedule assigning recipes to specific days.

## Shopping Item

An item currently needing purchase.

## Recently Purchased

A shopping item that has been marked as purchased.

Recently Purchased acts as:

- History
- Undo
- Quick Add

## Household

A group of users sharing data.

## Shopping Session

The process of purchasing items from the shopping list.

## Sync

The process of synchronizing local device changes with the backend.
