# Ingredo Project Plan

## Vision

Ingredo combines:

1. Meal Planning
2. Ingredient Management
3. Shared Shopping Lists
4. Recipe Management

into a single mobile-first experience.

## Core User Flow

Plan meals
↓
Generate ingredient needs
↓
Generate shopping list
↓
Shop
↓
Purchased items move into Recently Purchased

## Core Principle

Shopping items are not tasks.

Avoid checkbox-based shopping lists.

Purchased items move to:

- Recently Purchased
- Shopping History
- Quick Re-add Area
- Undo Area

## MVP

### Included

- Recipe management
- Recipe scaling
- Unit conversion
- Weekly meal planning
- Shopping list generation
- Manual shopping items
- Recently purchased section
- SQLite storage

### Excluded

- Authentication
- Households
- Sync
- Realtime updates

## Future Features

### Household Sharing

Shared:

- Recipes
- Meal Plans
- Shopping Lists

### Smart Suggestions

Use:

- Shopping history
- Meal plans
- Frequently purchased items

to suggest future shopping items.
